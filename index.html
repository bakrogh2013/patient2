Patient Call Simulation

1) Start the recording before beginning the patient call. (Note: You may have to enable or allow access to your microphone)

2) Click the phone icon to start the call with the patient.

3) When you are finished talking to the patient, click the phone icon to hang up and then click "stop recording".

4) Click "Download Recording"

5) Upload your recording to the Canvas assignment for you instructor to grade.
